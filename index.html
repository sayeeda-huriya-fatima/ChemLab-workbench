Determination of Hardness of Water by EDTA Method

Calculation of Total, Permanent, and Temporary hardness expressed as CaCO₃ equivalents (mg/L or ppm) using Eriochrome Black-T (EBT) indicator and standard EDTA solution.

50 mL Aliquot Basis
1. Total Hardness of Sample Water: Formula: (V₂ / V₁) × 1000 mg/L
= (23.2 / 18.5) × 1000 = 1254.05 mg/L (ppm CaCO₃ eq)
2. Permanent Hardness (Non-Carbonate Hardness): Formula: (V₃ / V₁) × 1000 mg/L
= (11.4 / 18.5) × 1000 = 616.22 mg/L (ppm CaCO₃ eq)
3. Temporary Hardness (Carbonate Hardness): Formula: Total - Permanent Hardness
= 1254.05 - 616.22 = 637.83 mg/L (ppm CaCO₃ eq)
